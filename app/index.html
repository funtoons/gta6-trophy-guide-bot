    About this guide

    Honest sourcing. Names, descriptions and grades are the official list as Exophase and PSNProfiles published it. Every guide is written in our own words and names the source each fact comes from — a source is cited, never copied. Five trophies had no confirmed method at launch (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin); this guide says so instead of inventing steps.

    No online trophies. All 55 trophies are single-player. The “Online” filter exists to show that plainly: it always returns an empty list.
